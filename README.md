## ✧ About the Movie

My favorite movie is **Forgotten (2017)**, a South Korean psychological thriller directed by Jang Hang-jun, starring Kang Ha-neul and Kim Mu-yeol.

## Movie Details

- **Title:** Forgotten
- **Release Year:** 2017
- **Country:** South Korea
- **Genre:** Psychological Thriller, Mystery, Crime, Drama
- **Director:** Jang Hang-jun

## ♡ Why I Love This Movie

I love *Forgotten* because of its unpredictable storyline and mind-blowing plot twists. It keeps me curious and makes me question everything happening in the movie. I also love how the suspense builds up throughout the story.
